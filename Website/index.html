FAQ

Questions from labs and dentists

What does Let It Flow do?

Let It Flow cleans digital dental impressions automatically. It removes noise and soft-tissue artefacts and reveals the cervical margin, so the technician gets a model ready for crown design.

Which scanners and software does it work with?

We connect to the cloud platforms of several major intraoral scanner brands. The cleaned model opens in your usual dental CAD software.

Does it change my original scan?

No. The original scan is always kept. You can compare the result with it and accept or reject each correction.

Who is it for?

Dental laboratories first, and dentists who design restorations in their practice. Orthodontic analysis is on our roadmap.

When is it available, and how much does it cost?

The beta opens on June 15, 2027. Each product will be sold as a monthly or yearly subscription, with a 14-day free trial on your own scans. Book a demo to join the first testers.
